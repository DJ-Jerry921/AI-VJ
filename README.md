# AI-VJ 🎧

### Bringing Professional Visual Experiences to Every DJ

AI-VJ is an AI-powered visual assistant designed to help student and amateur DJs create synchronized visual effects during live performances.

## 💡 Why I Started This Project

As a DJ, I have always been interested in how music and visuals work together.

After watching professional nightclub performances, I realized that visual effects are not just decorations. They are an important part of the audience's experience.

However, many student and amateur DJs do not have access to professional VJs or the time to prepare complex visual content.

My goal is to make these experiences more accessible through AI.

## 🚀 How AI-VJ Will Work

AI-VJ is being designed around three main features:

### 1. AI Visual Preparation
AI will help generate or prepare visual content before a performance, reducing the time DJs spend searching for videos.

### 2. Real-Time Music Analysis
The software will analyze live audio and eventually identify musical sections such as build-ups, breakdowns, and drops.

### 3. Automatic Visual Adaptation
AI-VJ will automatically select and adjust visual content as the music changes, allowing DJs to focus on mixing.

The long-term goal is to combine AI-prepared visuals with real-time music analysis.

## 💻 Current Development Progress

### ✅ V0.2 — Frequency Analysis
